# Face ID MAX - Quick Start Guide 🚀

## Get Started in 30 Seconds

### 1️⃣ Open App
**Link:** https://yapdru.github.io/Face-ID

### 2️⃣ Install on Phone (Optional)
**iPhone/iPad:**
- Tap Share → "Add to Home Screen"
- Looks like a native app!

**Android:**
- Menu → "Install app"
- Full-screen experience

### 3️⃣ Enroll Your Face
1. Tap **📷 Scan**
2. Enter your name
3. Tap **👤 Enroll**
4. Center face in green circle
5. Hold steady (15 frames)
6. Rotate to capture all angles (↑↓←→)
7. ✓ Done!

### 4️⃣ Login
1. Tap **🔓 Login**
2. Look at camera
3. **Blink once** to verify liveness
4. ✓ Unlocked!

## Tips for Best Results 💡

- **Lighting:** Good natural light
- **Distance:** 12-18 inches from camera
- **Remove:** Sunglasses, hats, masks
- **Hold Still:** Don't move during enrollment
- **Rotate:** Enroll from 4 angles for better recognition

## Troubleshooting 🔧

**Models won't load?**
- Check internet connection
- Refresh page
- Clear browser cache

**Face not recognized?**
- Better lighting
- Re-enroll at different angles
- Remove obstructions

**Not detecting face?**
- Camera permissions granted? ✓
- Face centered in circle
- Good lighting required
